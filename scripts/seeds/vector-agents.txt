Vector Agents (Lilian) — Product Intelligence Seed

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Vector Agents (brand name: Lilian) is an AI-powered Sales Development Representative (SDR) platform. It deploys autonomous AI agents that perform outbound sales tasks: prospecting, researching leads, writing personalized cold emails, following up, and booking meetings — without human intervention.

The core value proposition is replacing or augmenting human SDR teams with AI agents that work 24/7, never get tired, and can personalize outreach at scale using real-time signals from LinkedIn, company news, job postings, and funding events.

TARGET CUSTOMER
- B2B SaaS companies with outbound sales motions
- Series A to Series C startups scaling their GTM
- Revenue leaders, VP Sales, Head of Growth
- Companies with ACV $10k–$200k looking to scale pipeline without headcount
- Industries: fintech, HR tech, devtools, martech, revenue operations

CORE FEATURES
1. Autonomous AI SDR agents that research, write, and send personalized outreach
2. Multi-signal prospecting: tracks LinkedIn activity, job postings, funding announcements, tech stack changes
3. Personalized email + LinkedIn sequences generated by AI
4. Meeting booking integration (Calendly, HubSpot, Salesforce)
5. Real-time intent signal detection (website visits, G2 reviews, competitor mentions)
6. Campaign analytics and A/B testing built-in
7. Human-in-the-loop approval mode (optional)

PRICING MODEL
Usage-based + seat hybrid:
- Starter: ~$500/month for 1 agent, 500 contacts/month
- Growth: ~$1,500/month for 3 agents, 2,000 contacts/month
- Scale: ~$4,000/month for 10 agents, 10,000 contacts/month
- Enterprise: custom pricing for unlimited agents

COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE
Direct competitors:
- AiSDR: AI SDR focused on email personalization, $750–$2,000/month
- Artisan AI (Ava): AI SDR agent with LinkedIn + email, Series A funded
- 11x.ai: AI workers for GTM, raised $50M Series B in 2024
- Regie.ai: AI for sales content and sequences, more copilot than autonomous
- Clay: data enrichment + AI outreach, popular but requires human oversight
- Outreach / Salesloft: legacy SEPs adding AI features, expensive, complex
- Apollo.io: data + email sequences, large SMB base, adding AI

Key differentiators vs competitors:
- Fully autonomous (not just AI-assisted) — agents run end-to-end without human
- Multi-channel from day one (email + LinkedIn)
- Signal-driven: triggers outreach based on real-time buying intent signals
- Faster time-to-pipeline than human SDRs (live in 48 hours vs weeks to hire/train)

MARKET CONTEXT
The AI SDR market is accelerating rapidly in 2025-2026:
- Traditional SDR hiring is slowing as companies seek efficiency
- VC investment in AI GTM tools exceeded $2B in 2024
- Gartner predicts 75% of B2B sales orgs will use AI SDR tools by 2027
- Key concern: email deliverability (spam filters, inbox providers cracking down)
- Enterprise procurement moving toward AI-first vendor selection
- Average SDR costs $80k–$120k/year fully loaded; AI agents replace at ~5–10% of cost

CUSTOMER WINS / POSITIONING
Common reasons customers choose Vector Agents / Lilian:
- Tired of SDR churn (average SDR tenure 14 months)
- Need to scale pipeline without headcount approval
- Want consistency in messaging quality
- Interested in testing outbound in new markets/verticals quickly

Common objections:
- "Will AI outreach feel spammy?" — personalization engine addresses this
- "What about deliverability?" — dedicated sending infrastructure, domain warm-up
- "Does it integrate with our CRM?" — yes, Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive native
- "Will prospects know it's AI?" — optional disclosure settings
