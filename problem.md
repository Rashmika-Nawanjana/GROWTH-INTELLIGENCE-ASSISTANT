HACKATHON · PROBLEM STATEMENT From Signal to Action Growth teams have never had better data. They've rarely been worse at acting on it fast enough. DURATION 3–4 weeks TRACK Agents & Autonomous Systems TEAM SIZE Open vectoragents.ai 01 The Problem Growth is a loop — but most teams run it in pieces, with different tools, different people, and days or weeks between each step. Research lives in one place, content in another, outreach in a third, and performance data somewhere else entirely. By the time insight becomes action, it's stale. The signals that should drive campaigns — competitor moves, market shifts, audience sentiment, pricing patterns, channel trends — are scattered across the web and rarely connected to the content being created or the outreach being sent. Growth teams are executing on instinct, not intelligence. 02 The Question This Challenge Is Asking “What would it look like if a growth team could go from market signal to live campaign — researching, creating, personalising, deploying, and refining — inside a single conversation, without switching tools or losing context?” The gap is not intelligence — it's the distance between signal and action. The challenge is to close it. 03 The Full Growth Loop — Core Requirement The system must close the loop. Each stage feeds the next; real-world signal feeds back in. 01 Market intelligence Competitive signals, market shifts, audience language, channel trends — the live foundation everything else is built on › 02 Content generation Findings converted into outreach copy, posts, flyers, briefs — grounded in signal, not generic templates › 03 Multi-channel outreach Content deployed with A/B variants generated from the start — testing angles before scaling › 04 Feedback & signals Engagement, response rates, what resonated — captured and fed back into the intelligence layer › 05 Refined intelligence Each cycle starts sharper than the last — the system learns from real-world results ↺ Feedback loop — real-world engagement and response data flows back into stage 01, making the next cycle sharper Each cycle produces better intelligence, more targeted content, and higher-converting outreach — not because the model is smarter, but because the system accumulates real signal from the real world. 04 The Intelligence Problem Space The research layer must answer the questions that actually drive growth decisions. Market & category signals What narratives is the market responding to, PESTEL signals — and what's losing traction? Competitive intelligence How competitors are positioning, what they're saying, where the gaps are Audience & intent signals Who's in-market, what they care about, in their own words Channel & campaign intelligence What's working where, and how to allocate effort across channels Win/loss & conversion signals Why outreach converts or doesn't, and what to change in the next cycle Adjacent threats & opportunities What's coming from outside the current frame that will affect growth Contextual & temporal signals Seasons, buying cycles, global & local events, disruptions, special moments — the world around the campaign shapes whether the message lands 05 Content Generation & Outreach Intelligence without execution is just research. Every output must be traceable back to a signal. Personalised outreach sequences Cold outreach, follow-ups, re-engagement — written from competitive and audience signal, with A/B variants generated from the start, not retrofitted later Social & short-form content LinkedIn posts, threads, short-form copy — multiple angles generated simultaneously so the best framing can be tested against real engagement Visual artifacts Flyers, comparison graphics, infographics — generated as downloadable artifacts directly from intelligence surfaced, not designed from scratch Campaign briefs & documents One-pagers, positioning guides, strategy docs — structured outputs a team can act on without further synthesis 06 What “Ephemeral Interfaces” Means OUTPUT → USER When the system has findings, campaign variants, or A/B results to show, they materialise as purpose-built interfaces inside the conversation — a variant comparison grid, a channel performance map, a prospect list. No tool to open. No link to follow. e.g. two outreach variants rendered side-by-side — subject lines, opening hooks, CTAs — ready to select and deploy, inline. CLARIFICATION → USER When the system needs to narrow scope — which segment, which channel, which angle — it renders a small interactive UI in the thread. The user responds by clicking, not typing, keeping campaign momentum. Which channel should this go out on first? LinkedIn Email Both The conversation thread is the campaign workspace. Research, content, outreach, A/B results, and refined next steps — all in one place, no context lost. 07 Hard Constraints Multi-agent architecture Not just one LLM call Dynamic UIs in conversation Tool / MCP usage Live signal, not training data Full loop — not just research Not a chatbot Research findings, campaign variants, A/B results should materialise as interfaces inside the conversation — not pasted text, not links to open elsewhere. Not a single model call The loop requires multiple coordinated steps: researching, generating, testing, capturing feedback, refining. Each stage is a distinct process, not a single prompt. Live signal, not training data The intelligence layer must go out and look. Campaign content must be grounded in what's actually happening now — competitor messaging, audience signals, channel trends. The loop must close It's not enough to research and generate. The system must take feedback — engagement data, response patterns, what resonated — and feed it back to sharpen the next cycle. 08 Technical Concepts Worth Thinking About Not prescriptions — the problem space strong solutions tend to grapple with. 8.1 Signal source diversity Competitor ads & content, audience forums, job postings, funding activity, search trends, campaign engagement — each source type reveals a different growth truth. PESTEL Data that would be relevant 8.2 Parallelism & deep research Different dimensions investigated simultaneously. Multi-hop research that follows threads. Bounded depth budgets. Sub-investigations when a thread branches. 8.3 Control, lifecycle & failure State tracking across loop stages. Graceful degradation when threads fail. Clear handoffs between research, generation, outreach, and feedback stages. 8.4 Intent detection & mode switching The conversation moves naturally from research to generation to A/B to feedback to refinement — the system detects the shift, the user doesn't have to declare it. 8.5 Structured outputs & memory Typed findings (not freeform text) so the content layer knows its source. Confidence levels per claim. Campaign history that accumulates and informs each next cycle. 8.6 A/B logic & feedback ingestion Variants generated with testable hypotheses. Engagement signal interpreted against those hypotheses. The intelligence layer updated based on what the real world said. 08.4 The Loop in Action — Intent Detection The conversation moves between stages naturally. The system detects the shift; the user doesn’t declare it. INTENT → MODE “What’s the current positioning gap in the AI SDR market?” research “Now write three outreach variants targeting VP Sales at Series B companies” generate “Give me a version leading with competitor gap and one leading with ROI” A/B variants “The ROI angle got 3× the reply rate” loop back “Generate the next sequence using that refined angle” refined cycle “Actually, what’s changed in competitor messaging this week?” research The conversation holds the entire campaign cycle. No tool switching.
