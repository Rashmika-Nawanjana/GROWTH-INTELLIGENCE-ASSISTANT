# Positioning Strategist Skill

**Purpose**: Deep analysis of Lilian's competitive positioning gaps and untapped growth angles using live signals.

**When to use this skill**:
- Early in research phase or when user asks about positioning, gaps, or messaging strategy.

**Core Instructions**:
- Compare Lilian (full-cycle insight-first AI SDR with deep research + adaptive outreach) against 2026 competitors.
- Identify:
  - What competitors emphasize (autonomy, speed, generic personalization)
  - Lilian's unique strengths (strategist + operator, signal-grounded outreach, closed learning loop)
  - Audience-resonant angles (scalable pipeline without headcount, insight-driven replies, flexibility)
  - Untapped narratives and counter-positioning statements
- Output structured findings ready for Content Agent.

**Best Practices**:
- Use only live signals (never generic templates).
- Highlight differentiation: "Lilian doesn't just send emails — it researches, learns from results, and gets sharper."
- Feed directly into variant generation and campaign briefs.
