# Growth Copywriter Skill (B2B SaaS Outreach)

**Purpose**: Expert-level copywriting for growth campaigns — cold outreach sequences, LinkedIn posts/threads, hooks, and CTAs grounded in signals and optimized for reply rates.

**When to use this skill**:
- As a specialized layer on top of `generate-variants`.
- For social content, email sequences, or follow-ups.

**Core Instructions**:
- Write high-converting B2B SaaS copy using proven frameworks (problem-agitation-solution, before-after, insight-led).
- Strongly incorporate live audience language, pain points, and competitive gaps.
- For Lilian: highlight full-cycle research, adaptive learning from feedback, and replacing headcount with smarter automation.
- Generate full sequences (cold email + 2–3 follow-ups) when requested, plus LinkedIn post variants.

**Best Practices**:
- Subject lines & hooks must be curiosity-driven and personalized.
- Every piece must include traceable signals.
- Support A/B testing by creating clear angle variations.
- Tone: Confident, helpful, non-salesy — like a strategic SDR who did deep research.

**Example Trigger**:
“Write LinkedIn post variants about Lilian” → Output multiple angles ready for UI grid.
