# Humanizer & Natural Copy Skill

**Purpose**: Refine AI-generated outreach and content to sound authentically human, strategic, and high-quality — matching Lilian’s positioning of “human-quality replies” while staying grounded in signals.

**When to use this skill**:
- After `generate-variants` or any content generation.
- When output feels too robotic or generic.

**Core Instructions**:
- Take raw variants or copy and make them warmer, more conversational, and natural while preserving all signal-grounded elements and hypotheses.
- Remove corporate jargon, vary sentence length, add subtle personality, and improve flow.
- Maintain traceability: every claim or angle must still link back to research signals.
- Target tone: Professional yet approachable, strategist + operator (thoughtful but action-oriented).

**Best Practices**:
- Focus on B2B SaaS cold outreach style: clear value, empathy with pain points, specific personalization.
- Output both original and humanized versions side-by-side when useful for comparison.
- Ensure the final copy still supports strong A/B testing (distinct angles remain clear).
